FAQ

What does it do?
Watches RSS and Atom feeds and posts new entries to {{range $i, $k := .Data.Kinds}}{{if $i}}, {{end}}{{$k.Label}}{{end}}.
What address do I add?
The feed's, or the site's. A page that links to a feed resolves to that feed.
Will it post entries I already have?
No. Only entries that appear after you add the feed.
How often is a feed checked?
Every {{.Data.Interval}}, flat. Only the feed's own server can push a check later.
How many feeds and destinations?
{{.Data.MaxFeeds}} feeds and {{.Data.MaxDestinations}} destinations per account, any mix. Your Mastodon account is connected at sign-in.
Which feed posts where?
Each feed's page has a checkbox per destination. New feeds and new destinations start fully connected.
More than one Mastodon account?
Yes. Connect → Mastodon, name the instance, sign in to that account. Removing it revokes the authorisation.
What if a feed publishes a lot at once?
At most {{.Data.MaxItems}} entries per check go out. The rest are marked seen.
Why sign in with Mastodon?
It is the account you post from. Any instance works; your password never reaches feedrepeater.
Discord and Slack?
Channel webhook URL, stored encrypted and never shown again. Discord is checked silently; Slack gets one test line.
Bluesky?
An app password, not your account password. Revoke it in Bluesky any time.
ntfy?
A topic on ntfy.sh or your own server. Connecting publishes one notification. A token is only needed for reserved topics.
linkding?
Your instance's address and a REST API token. Entries become bookmarks; post text is the description.
Webhooks?
Signed JSON POST, HMAC-SHA256, secret shown once. The URL must echo a challenge before it is saved.
Post text?
Per destination: {{"{{title}} {{url}} {{summary}} {{author}} {{feed_title}} {{published}}"}}. Trimmed to each service's limit; the link is never cut. Settings holds the default for new destinations.
Timezone?
UTC unless set in Settings. Affects dates shown here and {{"{{published}}"}}.
When something fails?
Posts are retried, then get a Retry button. Re-connecting an account repairs a revoked authorisation; for your own, sign out and in. A feed failing for two weeks is stopped, with the reason shown.
What is stored?
Feeds, their entries, delivery history and encrypted credentials. Deleting your account removes all of it and revokes authorisations.
Cost?
Nothing during the beta.
